Personal Reflection

This section reflects on my career goals and experiences, and where I would like to take the direction of my career professionally!

From the time I was very young, I was always interested in technology. I was very tech literate very early on in my life, and I played around with simple code starting around 10 years old. And since then, I have continuously grown in my passion for computer science. I have always been interested in how computer programs work, solving problems through code, and making a real difference in the world through the software development world. As I have gone through college, it has only solidified me and made me more confident that computer science and data science are what I want to pursue and I have gotten a better idea of what I really enjoy about the field. I enjoy the challenge of it all. Programming and debugging can be very difficult sometimes, but it is also extremely rewarding when I can go from nothing to a fully working program or feature.

Through my coursework at Mizzou, I have gained an understanding of some crucial computer science concepts that I had mostly skipped in earlier years of learning to program - algorithms, data structures, and gaining data insights through statistical software and languages. Algorithms and data structures specifically are some of the most core differentiating skillsets between those with knowledge in computer science and just knowing programming language syntax, and understanding insights from datasets has given me more of a breadth in my skillset that I want to continue to grow. Aside from coursework, my personal projects have also helped me learn things much better than just hearing them in class because I learn by doing and creating in most cases. Looking back in my older pre-college projects, I can already see immense growth in my understanding and how I would do things differently now. I think that is a good sign because it has shown me that my skills and the way I approach problems are constantly continuing to improve.

My internship at DataServ has also given me a greater understanding of what a real- world development environment looks like. Before I had that opportunity, most of my experience in computer science was from both high school and college classes, a technical program I was a part of my junior year of high school that taught application programming, and personal projects. During the internship, I have gotten to work on real production systems primarily involving SQL databases, and I learned much more about the software development lifecycle and how development is done in the real world in a company environment. I also learned a lot more about Git version control, documentation, and how to gain an understanding of existing software systems (especially because before this, most of my experience was in writing programs on my own from scratch). I am also beginning to tutor CS students in Sept. 2026, which I believe will grow me even more and further develop skills in communicating CS concepts in an understandable way, which is very important in this field.

Looking ahead at my career, I am still figuring out exactly where I want to end up, but I am very interested in software engineering and data science. My main goals in college are to become a stronger programmer, become extremely AI proficient in software engineering, work on more advanced projects, gain more internship experience, and explore more areas of computer science I haven’t put much time into yet. I don’t expect to have everything figured out right now and I think my interests will definitely evolve over time. Ultimately, I want a career where I can keep constantly learning, solve interesting problems, and work on projects that are useful and make a real difference.
